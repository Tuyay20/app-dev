# My Favorite Movie: Tangled

## Why I Like Tangled
*Tangled* is my favorite Disney movie because it tells the story of **Rapunzel**, a brave and kind princess who dreams of seeing the floating lanterns. The movie is fun, inspiring, and full of adventure.

### About Rapunzel
- She is creative and loves painting.
- She is curious about the world outside her tower.
- She is brave enough to follow her dreams.
- She cares deeply about her friends and family.
